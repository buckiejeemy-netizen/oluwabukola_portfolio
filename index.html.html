About Me

A global health leader anchored in evidence, equity & impact

I am a public health professional with over 12 years of combined national and international experience spanning primary health care policy, program management, disease surveillance, health financing, and monitoring and evaluation. My career has taken me from Ministries of Health in Nigeria to WHO headquarters in Geneva — and now to Canada, where I continue to contribute to health equity and program excellence.

As a WHO Principal Investigator, I led multi-country research on acute care capacity and oxygen system readiness in low- and middle-income countries during the COVID-19 pandemic. My findings were published by WHO and adopted into global governance frameworks — a body of work I carry with deep professional pride.

I hold a Master of Public Health in Health Policy and Management, a PMP certification, and a Diploma in Human and Community Support Social Services from Cambrooks College in Calgary. I bring a systems-thinking approach, a strong command of data tools, and an unwavering commitment to improving health outcomes for vulnerable populations.
